Wordie

Spelling practice, in the shape of a Wordle ripoff built easier for younger kids. Six tries a word. Some letters come pre-filled, so there's already a foothold. Tap instead of type, and once you've found a letter, it locks in for every guess after. Confetti on every good tile.
