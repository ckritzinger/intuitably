# Carl Kritzinger

Engineer who builds products end-to-end and teams that last. I've launched products solo and at enterprise scale: FireWorks shipped 20+ products and spun out 3 independent startups, I started KnowBe4's innovation lab, built CryptoConvert as a side project and turned it into a standalone venture, and built an unauthorised app for Daily Maverick that they later acquired as their mobile flagship.

Sedgefield, South Africa, [ckritzinger@gmail.com](mailto:ckritzinger@gmail.com)

## Experience

**CTO & CPO, MoneyBadger**, 2023 - present
Co-founded South Africa's first provider of [retail cryptocurrency payments](https://cointelegraph.com/news/retail-giant-pick-n-pay-to-accept-bitcoin-in-1-628-stores-across-south-africa), enabling users of major crypto wallets (Binance, Luno, VALR, Bybit and others) to pay at thousands of merchants, including Pick n Pay, Flight Centre and Takealot. I led product, architecture and integrations across multiple PSPs and wallets, and designed and built most of our core payment, pricing and liquidation engines. We raised $500k from P1 Ventures and grew the team to six, expanding our merchant and PSP footprint alongside exchange partnerships across VALR, Luno, Binance and Bybit. I led the pivot from retail crypto-spend to B2B/treasury and cross-border settlement once retail product growth slowed.

**Principal Engineer, KnowBe4 Inc**, March 2018 - March 2023
After our acquisition by KnowBe4, my team and I rebuilt their core learner interface. I then recruited and led four teams to overhaul KnowBe4's training platform and content management systems, supporting millions of monthly active users. I started a research group to explore new product opportunities and foster a data-driven product management approach, co-authored a [patent](https://uspto.report/patent/grant/10,949,760), and wrote an 11-part animated video series on secure development practices.

**CTO, Popcorn Training**, June 2016 - February 2018
Brought my own platform IP into Popcorn Training and built it out as CTO on a revenue-share. We created a bespoke multi-tenant training platform (Rails, VueJS, Postgres), growing it to hundreds of thousands of registered users, with clients including banks, telcos, insurance and government. The platform's success contributed to the company's acquisition by KnowBe4 in 2018.

**Founder/CEO, FireWorks Innovation**, January 2013 - March 2016
Established FireWorks to build new fintech products, launching over 20 products and spinning out three separate startups. Clients included Standard Bank, Sanlam Investments and Naspers. FireWorks was acquired by OfferZen in 2016.

**Co-Founder, FireID 2.0**, February 2011 - December 2012
Partner in the management buyout of FireID Security and the rebuild of the company. I led product development for a series of banking and payment products. FireID incubated (amongst others) SnapScan, Luno, Mxit Money, and an identity management product later [acquired by ESET](https://www.eset.com/my/business/solutions/identity-and-data-protection/).

**Developer, FireID Security**, 2008 - 2010
Began as a contract developer, progressing to engineering team lead and roles spanning product management, engineering culture and sales engineering. Grew the engineering team from 4 to 20 members.

**Freelance Developer**, 2006 - 2007
Numerical modelling and signal processing work for a variety of clients.
