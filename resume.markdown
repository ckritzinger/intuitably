Engineer and founder with a habit of building durable products and teams. Now building MoneyBadger. Previously FireWorks (acquired by OfferZen), Popcorn Training (acquired by KnowBe4), an unauthorised Daily Maverick app (acquired by Daily Maverick), and the platform that incubated SnapScan and Luno.

Sedgefield, South Africa, [ckritzinger@gmail.com](mailto:ckritzinger@gmail.com)

## Experience

**CTO & CPO, MoneyBadger**, (2023 - present)

Co-founded South Africa's first provider of [retail cryptocurrency payments](https://cointelegraph.com/news/retail-giant-pick-n-pay-to-accept-bitcoin-in-1-628-stores-across-south-africa), enabling users of major crypto wallets (Lightning network, Binance, Luno, VALR, Bybit and others) to pay at thousands of merchants, including Pick n Pay, Flight Centre and Takealot. I designed and built the payment, pricing and liquidation engines processing integrations 8 PSPs. We were the infrastructure behind every crypto payment at Pick n Pay, Flight Centre and Takealot.

**Principal Engineer, KnowBe4 Inc**, (March 2018 - March 2023)

After KnowBe4 acquired Popcorn Training, I led the team that rebuilt the learner interface serving several million monthly active users. I recruited and led four teams to overhaul KnowBe4's training platform and content management systems, started a research group to promote rapid prototyping, co-authored [patent #10,949,760](https://uspto.report/patent/grant/10,949,760), and wrote an 11-part animated secure-development video series.

**CTO, Popcorn Training**, (June 2016 - February 2018)

Brought my own platform IP into Popcorn Training and built it out as CTO on a revenue-share. We created a bespoke multi-tenant training platform (Rails, VueJS, Postgres), growing it to hundreds of thousands of registered users, with clients including banks, telcos, insurance and government. The platform's success contributed to the company's acquisition by KnowBe4 in 2018.

**Founder/CEO, FireWorks Innovation**, (January 2013 - March 2016)

Established FireWorks to build new fintech products, launching over 20 products and spinning out three separate startups. Clients included Standard Bank, Sanlam Investments and OLX (Naspers). FireWorks was acquired by OfferZen in 2016.

**Co-Founder, FireID 2.0**, (February 2011 - December 2012)

Partner in the management buyout of FireID Security. Built the identity and payments IP that incubated SnapScan, Luno and Mxit Money, plus a separate identity-management product later [acquired by ESET](https://www.eset.com/my/business/solutions/identity-and-data-protection/).

**Developer, FireID Security**, (2008 - 2010)

Joined as a contract developer, grew into engineering lead. I helped take the team from 4 to 20 while shipping FireID's core banking security products.
